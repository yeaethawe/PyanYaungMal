Pyan Yaung Mal

Resell · Renew · Reuse — buy and sell used products in Myanmar. The app itself is only available on a local network.

KBZ Pay Wave Pay QR + chat

Resell

List what you no longer need with photos, a description, and a price.

Renew

Give items a second life instead of buying everything new.

Reuse

Pay with KBZ Pay or Wave Pay QR — no in-app purchase required.

Local network only

Pyan Yaung Mal is not a public website. It runs on a computer on your Wi‑Fi. Phones and other devices can open it only when they are on that same local network. This Netlify page is an overview — it cannot sign you in, list products, or open chat.

How it works

  1. Create an account first, then start buying or selling. You can sign in to more than one account and switch between them.
  2. Sellers upload a KBZ Pay or Wave Pay QR code, one or more product photos, a description, and a price.
  3. When someone buys, the seller is notified in chat.
  4. After payment, the seller marks the listing sold out, and it leaves Explore.

Why QR payments

In-app purchases are not available in some parts of Myanmar. Payment happens with a QR code and a screenshot in chat.
