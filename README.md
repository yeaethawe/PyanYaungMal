# PyanYaungMal

Resell · Renew · Reuse. A marketplace for buying and selling used products in Myanmar.

Create an account first, then buy or sell. Sellers upload a KBZ Pay or Wave Pay QR code, product photos, a description, and a price. When someone buys, the seller is notified in chat; after payment, they mark the listing sold out and it leaves Explore.

In-app purchases are not available in some parts of Myanmar, so payment happens with a QR code and a screenshot in chat. You can also sign in to more than one account and switch between them.
