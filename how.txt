Users must create account first then start buying or selling their products.

For selling they need to upload their payment QR codes first which is K pay or Wave pay.
Then they need to upload their product photo one photo or multiple photos.
Then they need to fill the detail discription and pricing of their product.

After a user bought the product, the seller will be informed and let the seller to close selling the product after it is sold out.

User can also sign in new accounts and switch between accounts.

In Myanmar in app purchase is not available in some areas. That is why we use this system.
